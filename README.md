## 🔥 What Is Toka?

Toka is not a ticketing app.

That is the most important thing to understand about it.

Every other platform in this space — Eventbrite, Ticket Master, Billetto, and the dozens of others — built a ticketing tool and called it an events platform. You show up, you buy a ticket, you get a QR code, you go home. The platform played no meaningful role in your night. It does not know who you are, what you love, who you met, or whether you had the time of your life. It just took its cut and moved on.

**Toka is built around a completely different idea.**

What if the platform actually helped you have a better experience — before you even walked through the door? What if it knew what kind of events light you up, showed you things that genuinely match your interests, connected you with the people going to the same event so you are not arriving as a stranger, and remembered your experience long after the night was over?

That is Toka. The social infrastructure for real-world experiences. A platform that treats every event — every concert, campus meetup, church conference, food fair, business summit, community cleanup, or underground art show — as a chance to bring people together in a way that actually means something.

**Built in Uganda. Designed for Africa. Ready for everywhere.**

## 🌍 The Problem Nobody Has Solved

<br/>

Walk into any event in Kampala, Mbarara, Nairobi, Lagos, or Accra right now. Ask the organiser how they collected registrations. More likely than not, they will describe a combination of WhatsApp group chats, manual spreadsheets, Mobile Money screenshots forwarded to personal phones, and a prayer that the right people show up. The tools that exist were not built for this market. They assume credit cards. They assume fast, stable internet. They assume the world looks like San Francisco.

It does not.

And for the people who actually do use the international platforms — they pay for it. Fees of 8%, 10%, sometimes 30% added silently on top of the ticket price. A music event that should cost UGX 20,000 ends up costing UGX 27,000 by the time a foreign platform is done with it. For small community organisers, independent artists, campus societies, and local entrepreneurs, those fees are simply not acceptable.

Beyond the money problem, there is a discovery problem. Right now, there is no reliable answer to the question: **"What is happening near me this weekend that I would actually enjoy?"** Google gives you a list. Facebook Events is a mess of sponsored posts and irrelevant recommendations. Nothing feels personal. Nothing feels curated. Nobody is helping you find your people.

And then there is the loneliness problem — the one nobody talks about. A huge number of people do not go to events they would love because they do not want to go alone. They do not know if anyone they know is going. They have no way to connect with fellow attendees before the night. So they stay home. The organiser loses a customer. The community loses a member. Everyone loses.

**Toka is the answer to all three of these problems at once.**

## ✨ What Makes Toka Different

<br/>

### 🎯 It Knows What You Love

When you join Toka, you spend two minutes telling it what you are into. Music genres. Sports. Faith. Food. Tech. Art. Entrepreneurship. The platform builds a living picture of your interests from what you select and what you actually attend. From that moment, your feed is not a generic list of every event within 50 kilometres. It is a curated stream of experiences that feel like they were picked for you — because they were.

<br/>

### 👥 Meet Your People Before You Arrive

This is the feature that does not exist anywhere else.

When you register for an event on Toka, you get access to a **pre-event community space** — a lightweight, purpose-built gathering of everyone attending that specific event. You can see who else is coming, discover shared interests between yourself and other attendees, ask questions, make plans, and arrive already knowing people. The loneliness of going somewhere alone disappears before the night even starts. You are not a stranger walking into a crowd. You are part of something that has already begun.

For organisers, this is transformative. An engaged, connected audience before the event means better energy on the day, higher attendance rates, and people who feel genuine ownership over the experience.

<br/>

### 📸 The Night Lives On

After every other platform, the event disappears. It happened. It is over. Nothing remains.

Toka creates a **post-event space** automatically — a shared memory of what just happened. Photos gathered from attendees. Connections made during the night that can be preserved with a single tap. Short voice or text reflections: *"What was your favourite moment?"* Organisers get honest, textured feedback. Attendees get a memory they can return to. And the data gets smarter, so your next recommendation is even more perfectly matched to what you actually love.

<br/>

### 📱 Mobile Money First. Always.

This is the infrastructure decision that changes everything.

Registration and payment on Toka works with **MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, and M-Pesa** as the primary payment method — from day one, not as an afterthought. No bank account required. No credit card required. A student, a market vendor, a smallholder farmer, a boda boda rider — anyone with a mobile money wallet can register for any event on Toka in under a minute.

For free events, the friction is essentially zero. Enter your phone number. Get an SMS confirmation. Show up. The platform works even on a basic phone with limited internet because your confirmation travels by text.

This is not a feature. It is a philosophy. It means Toka is genuinely for everyone — not just the banked, the connected, and the already-privileged.

<br/>

### 📊 Organisers Finally Know Their Audience

Running events on Toka gives you something no other platform offers — genuine intelligence about the people who show up for you.

Not just a headcount. Not just a revenue figure. But a real picture: where your audience comes from, what other kinds of events they love, which message brought them to you, what they were most excited about before the event, and who your most loyal fans are — the ones who have shown up three times, four times, five times. Those people deserve to be recognised. Toka helps you find them, reward them, and bring them back.

<br/>

### 🔗 Every Attendee Becomes a Promoter

Every ticket on Toka comes with a personal referral link. Share it. If someone registers through your link, you earn credit — early access, discounts, rewards that the organiser chooses. Word of mouth, which is how events actually spread in African cities, gets captured and rewarded by the platform instead of happening invisibly outside it.

## 💸 How Toka Makes Money (Eventually)

<br/>

Toka is free first. Always.

Community events, campus meetups, church gatherings, neighbourhood activities — completely free to list, completely free to attend, forever. This is not a temporary promotion. It is a permanent commitment to the community that makes Toka worth using.

When organisers run paid events, Toka takes a small, clearly communicated percentage — significantly lower than any major competitor, always charged to the organiser, never hidden from the attendee. The price you see is the price you pay.

As Toka grows, organisers who run frequent events will have access to premium tools — deeper audience insights, early-bird ticket mechanics, marketing tools built into the platform, and the ability to build loyal communities around their brand across every event they run.

**The goal is simple: be the most honest, most useful, and most fairly priced events platform that has ever existed in this market.**

## 🛠️ Run It Yourself

<br/>

Toka's MVP is intentionally simple. No complicated setup. No special software. Just three steps:

```bash
# 1. Get the code
git clone https://github.com/yourusername/toka.git

# 2. Enter the folder
cd toka

# 3. Open in your browser
open index.html
```

That is it. Everything runs in the browser. No internet required after the first load. No accounts to create on other platforms. No API keys to configure. Just open and use.

## 🤝 Contributing

<br/>

Toka is being built in public. If you have attended an event and thought *"this could be better"* — your instinct is correct and your perspective matters here.

If you want to contribute, the best place to start is the **Issues** tab. Look for anything labelled `good first issue` or `community feedback`. You do not need to be an experienced developer to contribute meaningfully. Design feedback, user experience observations, local knowledge about how events work in your city, and ideas for features that would actually help real people — all of this is genuinely valuable.

**Specifically looking for:**
- 🇺🇬 People who organise events in Uganda (any size, any category)

- 🌍 Anyone who can speak to how events work in other African cities
